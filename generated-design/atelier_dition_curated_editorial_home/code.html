The Art of Unlined Weightlessness

Traditional tailoring relies on layers of stiff interlinings and horsehair canvas. At Atelier Édition, our partnered houses engineer structure directly into the yarn itself.

Using double-faced grade-A Inner Mongolian cashmere, master craftspeople carefully hand-split every edge along seamlines, folding the margins inward and securing them with 14 hours of invisible hand-blind stitching per garment.
